About

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Institute of Science Tokyo, working with Professor Katsuki Fujisawa on safe autonomous systems with reinforcement learning. My research sits at the intersection of nonlinear control theory, optimization, and learning, with the goal of making autonomous robots and robot teams provably safe in realistic operating conditions.

I received my Ph.D. in Control Systems and Automation from IIT Jodhpur in 2025, advised by Prof. Anoop Jain. My thesis introduced a Möbius-transformation-based barrier Lyapunov framework for robots whose safety margins vary with position, and extended it to phase-coordinated multi-agent systems and flow-field disturbances, with hardware experiments on Crazyflie 2.1 quadrotors and Khepera IV ground robots. Before that, I completed an M.Tech. at NIT Hamirpur and a B.Tech. at Bhilai Institute of Technology, both in Electrical Engineering.

Research

Robots rarely operate in symmetric, obstacle-free space. A drone circling inside a geofence, a patrol team covering a border with high-priority zones, or an underwater vehicle pushed by currents all face constraints that vary with position and time. My work develops barrier Lyapunov and control barrier function methods that handle this variability with formal guarantees, and validates them on hardware.

Research highlights

  • Geofencing with nonconcentric boundaries

    Circular motion control of a unicycle robot inside a geofence that is not centred on the desired orbit. A Möbius transformation maps the uneven margin to a uniform one, where the problem becomes either trajectory-constraining or obstacle-avoidance; the resulting control law depends only on actual-plane parameters.

    IEEE Trans. Automatic Control, 2026Khepera IV and Crazyflie 2.1 PaperarXivVideoCode

  • Phase-coordinated multi-agent motion

    Möbius phase-shift coupled synchronization and balancing. Agents evenly spaced in the transformed plane become unevenly spaced in the actual plane, so a team can concentrate coverage in regions that need intensified monitoring, such as zones near critical infrastructure on a patrolled border.

    Under review, IEEE TACFour-robot Khepera IV experiments arXivVideoCode

  • Coordination in spatiotemporal flow fields

    Extension to agents moving through smooth, bounded flows such as wind or ocean currents. Flow-induced motion enters the inertial-frame dynamics, and flow-aware BLF control laws keep the formation inside the boundary.

    ASME J. Mechanisms and Robotics, 2026 Paper

  • Formation control with collision avoidance and connectivity

    Distributed distance-based formation control using asymmetric time-varying BLFs. Agents are disks of different radii; the asymmetric barrier prevents collisions as agents approach and preserves communication links as they separate, with analytical bounds on all post-design signals.

    Systems & Control Letters, 2024; ACC 2022; ICC 2021 PaperCode

  • Constrained control beyond robotics

    The same barrier ideas keep signals inside prescribed, possibly asymmetric bounds in other systems: bounded voltage regulation in DC microgrids with uncertain load current, and constrained synchronization of chaotic systems with parametric uncertainty.

    IEEE Control Systems Letters, 2023; ACC 2024 L-CSS paperACC paper

Current work

  • Safe navigation with learning-augmented CLF–CBF control

    • Safe unicycle navigation among multiple obstacles under bounded disturbances, combining CLF–CBF quadratic programs with learning.
    • Robust multi-agent circumnavigation under bounded disturbances and unknown dynamic obstacles, with reinforcement learning (PPO).
    • Resilient formation control: RL-based detection and optimization-based mitigation of false data injection attacks.
    • Safe autonomous systems with reinforcement learning at Fujisawa Lab, Institute of Science Tokyo.
    Indian Control Conference 2026 (accepted)

  • Barrier Lyapunov control for safe LLM text generation

    Bringing barrier Lyapunov ideas from robotics to large language models: enforcing a strict safety margin during text generation, with generation treated as a controlled process.

    Indian Control Conference 2026 (accepted)With Anand Singh and Anjali Tiwari
